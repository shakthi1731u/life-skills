# Grit and Growth Mindset

## 1. Grit

Grit means having the patience and determination to keep working towards a goal, even when things get difficult. It is not just about being talented; it is also about putting in consistent effort and not giving up easily.

## 2. Introduction to Growth Mindset

A growth mindset means believing that we can improve our skills and abilities through learning and practice. Instead of being afraid of mistakes, we should treat them as opportunities to learn something new.

## 3. Internal Locus of Control

Internal locus of control means believing that our actions and decisions have an important role in shaping our results.

The main point I understood from the video is that instead of blaming situations or other people, we should focus on what we can control. Taking responsibility for our actions helps us find solutions and improve ourselves.

## 4. How to Build a Growth Mindset

The speaker shares these important points:

1. Believe that you can figure things out.
2. Question your assumptions instead of accepting everything as it is.
3. Create your own learning plan and keep improving your knowledge.
4. Accept the struggle because learning and improvement are not always easy.

## 5. My Ideas to Build a Growth Mindset

I can build a growth mindset by making a few changes in my daily routine:

1. **Learn something new every day:** Spend some time learning new things, even if it is just a small topic.
2. **Accept challenges:** Instead of avoiding difficult tasks, try to complete them and learn from the experience.
3. **Learn from mistakes:** When something goes wrong, understand what caused it and try not to repeat the same mistake.
4. **Ask for feedback:** Take suggestions from others and use them to improve myself.
5. **Take responsibility:** Stop blaming situations and focus on what I can do to make things better.
6. **Be consistent:** Work towards my goals regularly instead of depending only on motivation.
7. **Track my progress:** Compare my current skills with my previous skills to understand how much I have improved.
8. **Help others learn:** Share what I know with others because teaching can also improve my understanding.
9. **Be patient:** Understand that learning something new takes time and improvement will not happen overnight.
10. **Step outside my comfort zone:** Try things that I usually avoid because they seem difficult or unfamiliar.

I believe that developing a growth mindset is a continuous process. I may not improve every day, but I can make progress by learning from my mistakes, accepting challenges and putting in consistent effort.
