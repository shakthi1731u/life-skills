**Learning Process -- Answers**  
**Question 1: What is the Feynman Technique?**  
The Feynman Technique is a way of learning by explaining a topic in  
   
 simple words, as if you are teaching it to someone else.  
**Question 2: What was the most interesting story or idea for you in Barbara Oakley's video?**  
The most interesting part for me was Barbara Oakley's story about how  
   
 she struggled with maths and science when she was younger but later  
   
 learned these subjects and became an engineering professor. It showed me  
   
 that learning is not only about how talented we are. The way we learn  
   
 and the effort we put into it also matter.  
**Question 3: What are active and diffused modes of thinking?**  
- **Active mode:** This is when we focus closely on a problem and  
   
 think about it directly.  
- **Diffused mode:** This is when we relax and allow our mind to make  
   
 connections in the background. It can help us understand a problem  
   
 from a different angle.  
Both modes are useful when learning something new.  
**Question 4: According to the video, what are the steps to take when approaching a new topic?**  
- Break the skill into smaller parts.  
- Learn enough to identify and correct your mistakes.  
- Remove distractions and anything that makes practice difficult.  
- Set aside dedicated time to practise.  
- Practise for at least 20 hours.  
**Question 5: What are some actions you can take going forward to improve your learning process?**  
- I will try to understand the concept instead of just memorising the  
   
 answer.  
- I will explain what I learn in my own words to check whether I  
   
 really understand it.  
- I will practise by writing code and working on small tasks instead  
   
 of only watching videos.  
- I will keep my phone away and avoid social media while studying.  
- I will divide bigger topics into smaller parts and set a deadline  
   
 for each one.  
- If I get stuck, I will try to find the reason instead of immediately  
   
 looking for the solution.  
- I will review what I have learned and practise it regularly.
