**Listening and Active Communication**  
**1. Steps and Strategies for Active Listening**  
Active listening means giving full attention to the speaker and understanding the message before responding.  
1. **Pay attention:** Focus on the speaker and avoid distractions such as mobile phones.  
2. **Maintain eye contact:** Use natural eye contact to show that you are listening.  
3. **Use positive body language:** Nod when appropriate and keep an attentive posture.  
4. **Do not interrupt:** Allow the speaker to finish before giving your response.  
5. **Avoid judging too quickly:** Listen to the complete message before forming an opinion.  
6. **Ask questions:** Ask relevant questions when something is unclear.  
7. **Paraphrase:** Repeat the main idea in your own words to confirm your understanding.  
8. **Give feedback:** Respond in a way that shows you understood the speaker.  
**2. Key Points of Reflective Listening According to Fisher's Model**  
Reflective listening helps us understand both the speaker's words and feelings. Fisher's model includes four important qualities:  
- **Empathy:** Try to understand the speaker's feelings and point of view.  
- **Acceptance:** Listen without judging or immediately disagreeing.  
- **Congruence:** Be genuine and honest in your response.  
- **Concreteness:** Focus on clear and specific details instead of vague statements.  
A reflective listener also listens more than they speak, restates important points, asks for clarification, and checks whether they have understood the speaker correctly.  
**3. Obstacles in My Listening Process**  
Some obstacles that may affect my listening are:  
- I sometimes become distracted by my mobile phone or other activities.  
- I may start thinking about my reply while the other person is still speaking.  
- Long explanations can make it difficult for me to maintain concentration.  
- I may not understand some unfamiliar or technical words.  
- Sometimes I make assumptions before hearing the complete message.  
- I may forget some details if I do not pay enough attention.  
**4. How I Can Improve My Listening**  
I can improve my listening skills by following these steps:  
- Keep my mobile phone away during important conversations.  
- Give the speaker my full attention.  
- Avoid interrupting and wait until the speaker finishes.  
- Ask questions when I do not understand something.  
- Repeat the main points in my own words to confirm understanding.  
- Practise listening to English conversations, interviews, and podcasts.  
- Focus on the speaker's tone and body language, not only their words.  
**5. When I Switch to Passive Communication**  
I may use passive communication when I speak with someone who has more authority or experience than me. I may avoid sharing my opinion because I do not want to create an argument or hurt the other person's feelings. Sometimes I find it difficult to say no, even when I disagree or do not have time to help.  
**6. When I Switch to Aggressive Communication**  
I may become aggressive when I feel frustrated, when someone repeatedly interrupts me, or when I feel that my opinion is not being respected. Under pressure, I may respond quickly or raise my voice instead of explaining my point calmly. I should avoid blaming others and give myself time to respond.  
**7. When I Switch to Passive-Aggressive Communication**  
I may use passive-aggressive communication when I feel upset but do not want to discuss the problem directly. For example, I may stay silent, make sarcastic comments, or express my frustration indirectly. This can create confusion because I am not clearly explaining what is bothering me.  
**8. How I Can Make My Communication Assertive**  
I can become more assertive by expressing my thoughts, feelings, and needs clearly while respecting other people.  
- **Identify my feelings:** Understand what I am feeling before responding.  
- **Explain my needs:** State what I need in a clear way.  
- **Use “I” statements:** For example, “I feel uncomfortable when I am interrupted.”  
- **Speak respectfully:** Be direct without blaming, threatening, or insulting others.  
- **Learn to say no:** Refuse requests politely when necessary.  
- **Use confident body language:** Maintain a calm voice, suitable eye contact, and an open posture.  
- **Practise in simple situations:** Start by expressing my opinion in everyday conversations.  
- **Respect the other person:** Listen to their point of view even when I disagree..
