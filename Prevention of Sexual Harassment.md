# PoSH (Prevention of Sexual Harassment) 

## 1. Meaning of Sexual Harassment

Sexual harassment is any **unwanted behaviour of a sexual nature** that makes a person feel uncomfortable, threatened, or intimidated at the workplace.

## 2. Types of Sexual Harassment

| Type | Examples |
|---|---|
| Verbal | Sexual jokes, comments, inappropriate questions |
| Physical | Unwanted touching, hugging, kissing |
| Non-verbal | Staring, suggestive gestures, inappropriate looks |
| Visual | Sharing or displaying sexually explicit images |
| Online | Unwanted sexual messages, emails, or social media posts |
| Quid pro quo | Offering job benefits in exchange for sexual favours or threatening consequences for refusing |

## 3. What to Do If You Face Sexual Harassment

1. Tell the person to stop if you feel safe doing so.
2. Record the incident, including the date, time, and location.
3. Keep relevant evidence, such as messages, emails, or screenshots.
4. Report the incident to HR or the Internal Committee (IC).
5. Follow the company's PoSH complaint procedure.
6. Cooperate with the investigation.

## 4. What to Do If You Witness Sexual Harassment

1. Support the affected person.
2. Intervene only if it is safe to do so.
3. Report the incident to HR or the Internal Committee.
4. Share factual information if an investigation takes place.
5. Maintain confidentiality.
6. Do not blame, threaten, or discriminate against the affected person.

## 5. Important PoSH Terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| PoSH | Prevention of Sexual Harassment |
| IC | Internal Committee |
| ICC | Internal Complaints Committee (commonly Prevention of Sexual Harassmentused term) |
| Workplace | Office or other work-related locations and settings |
| Retaliation | Taking negative action against someone for reporting harassment or participating in an investigation |
| Confidentiality | Keeping complaint and investigation details private |

## 6. Key Points to Remember

- Consent and personal boundaries must be respected.
- Unwanted sexual behaviour should not be ignored.
- Sexual harassment can happen once or repeatedly.
- Everyone has a responsibility to maintain a safe workplace.
- Complaints should be handled fairly and confidentially.
- Retaliation against someone who reports harassment is not acceptable.
